## ✨ About

**ResuMate** is a complete resume toolkit for job seekers. Start from a polished template, write with AI assistance, then check how your resume scores against applicant tracking systems (ATS) and the exact job you want — before you apply.

## 🚀 Features

- 🛠️ **Resume Builder** — a full editor with live preview
- 🎨 **Templates gallery** — professional designs for every field
- 🎯 **ATS Score Checker** — see how your resume reads to hiring software
- ✨ **Resume Optimizer** — AI suggestions to strengthen every section
- 🔍 **Job Scan** — match your resume against a job description
- 🎤 **Interview Coach** — practise questions and get feedback
- 🤝 **Negotiation Coach** — prepare for the salary conversation
- 📂 **My Resumes dashboard** — all your saved resumes in one place
- 📄 **Samples** — ready-made example resumes to start from

## 🎮 How to Use

1. Open the live app and pick a template.
2. Fill in your details in the builder — the preview updates live.
3. Run the **ATS Checker** and **Job Scan** to fine-tune your resume.
4. Download your finished resume as a PDF.
